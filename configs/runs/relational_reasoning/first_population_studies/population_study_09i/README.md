# Study 09i: truth-target high-statistics persistence

This is the separate truth-target counterpart of Study 09h. The task, L=3
family, focused `rho={0.80,0.85}` crossover grid, strategic evidence, 15
repetitions, controller, prompts, retention, and analysis are matched. Only the controller target
semantics change: both the frozen truth and controller target are `NORTH`.

The 24 structural cells contain 360 target episodes. Receiver disposition is
fixed to naive and evidence selection is fixed to strategic.
At controlled updates the production runtime replaces one social slot, so q=1
has one controller source and q=2 has one controller plus one ordinary peer.

Analysis remains empirical-only and writes permanent CSV tables. It includes
eight-bin state-local chi/T_pi/eta_IR phase maps with explicit structural,
occupancy, and estimator-support states, adjacent occupancy maps, cell-level
signed/bounded eta_th diagnostics, late target/truth/active-phi outcomes, and
truth-takeover fractions. Eta_th is not invented at state-local resolution.
Matched tables and heatmaps report `q=2 - q=1` effects for every budget and
rho under strategic evidence. Rho-marginal tables are explicitly
descriptive summaries over only the two focused rho values.

Study 09e is only a reuse candidate for the eight overlapping q=2/strategic
cells at the four legacy budgets and two focused rho values, for at most 80
episodes. Every candidate must pass sealed-artifact and exact
scientific-identity validation before it can reduce the new-episode count.

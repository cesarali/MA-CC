# Study 09k: N=24 truth-target q=1, L=2 persistence

Study 09k is the larger-population, high-statistics truth-target successor to
Study 09g and the matched counterpart of Study 09j. It preserves `q=1`, `L=2`
and 30 rounds, but deliberately changes the epistemic class from
`naive_strategic` to `vigilant_strategic`.

The frozen task is `pop24_L2_r06/task_0002`. Both truth and controller target
are `SOUTHWEST`. Strategic evidence is always a real true frozen-task fact,
selected and citation-validated by the existing production implementation.

The controller target is absent from local-vote initialization. Study 09k can
therefore reuse the exact 20 complete physical initial states generated once
for the matched Study 09j/09k pair. No target fraction is prescribed; natural
variation is retained between repetitions and exactly paired across cells.

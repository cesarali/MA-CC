# Headline
The archive supports a clear, null-checked statement about endpoint target information for the SENSING policies, and a clear negative statement for the ALWAYS policies. It does NOT support a stable trajectory-cost estimate, and therefore only a handful of exploratory efficiency ratios (all small: 0.04 to 0.14). In this experiment the "truthful report" controller is mostly a target-independent disturbance: its trajectories are easy to tell apart from silence (cost scores of 1 to 8 nats) but carry little information about which target it was given.

# 1. Endpoint information I(Z; Y_h): present for sensing, absent for always
- Sensing policies, h = 10: the held-out information score is 0.15-0.34 nats in 7 of 8 cells (max possible log 2 = 0.693): q12/rho1.00/b12 0.338 [0.23, 0.44], q12/rho1.00/b3 0.295, q3/rho1.00/b12 0.268, q12/rho0.70/b3 0.239, q3/rho1.00/b3 0.222, q3/rho0.70/b3 0.147, q12/rho0.70/b12 0.074; q3/rho0.70/b12 is 0.041 with an interval touching zero. Held-out accuracy 0.75-0.90. All 8 sensing cells beat 200 whole-parent label swaps at h = 10 (p = 0.005, the smallest value 200 swaps can give).
- Always policies, h = 10: scores are within noise of zero in all 8 cells (-0.03 to +0.025), accuracy 0.45-0.63, and no cell beats the swap null convincingly (two at p = 0.015, six at p > 0.08).
- Information grows with horizon for sensing cells (near zero at h = 1, rising through h = 5 to h = 10); it stays near zero at every horizon for always cells.
- Model selection: the one-standard-error rule chose linear (63), quadratic (40), MLP (30) or constant (27) across the 160 endpoint jobs. Where information is present, quadratic logistic on (u, v) was usually enough; the MLP never won by a margin that mattered. Saturation of clipped probabilities was negligible (< 4%).
- The in-sample frequency plug-in is badly biased upward: mean 0.28 nats at h = 10 versus a held-out smoothed-density score of -0.39 (alpha = 1) and a held-out classifier score of 0.10. On the synthetic "identical" law with 40 parents the raw frequency estimate reads 0.31-0.34 nats where the truth is exactly zero. Do not quote the frequency number.

# 2. Why "always" carries no target information: the direction check
The controller may only post canonical TRUE facts chosen to favour its target. Under always-on posting, BOTH targets drive the population toward ALLOCATION_2 (the false answer). Example q12/rho0.70/b12 at h = 10: silent ends with 80% on ALLOCATION_0 (the correct answer); the target-0 branch ends with 95% on ALLOCATION_2; the target-2 branch with 98% on ALLOCATION_2. Paired gain toward the correct answer is NEGATIVE in 15 of 16 cells (-0.12 to -0.77 share) and gain toward the false target is positive in all 16 (0.15 to 0.84). This was cross-checked against the archive's own endpoint table (identical numbers). Whatever the mechanism (the selective true facts favouring allocation 0 appear to be read as evidence for allocation 2, or the mere presence of reports destabilises a converging population), the outcome is a large target-independent shift, so the two targets are indistinguishable at the endpoint. The sensing policies act less often, disturb less, and leave the target visible. This is exactly the synthetic "generic perturbation" scenario (I = 0, C > 0), and it means the efficiency numbers for the always policies are zero over something large.

# 3. Trajectory cost C: large, but not stably estimable
- Controlled and silent paths are easy to separate: held-out classification accuracy 0.85-1.00 for always cells and for every target-2 branch, 0.5-0.75 for sensing target-0 branches (which barely act once the group already agrees on allocation 0).
- The held-out NWJ scores (population lower bounds on KL) are 2-8 nats for always cells and target-2 sensing cells, and 0-2 nats for target-0 sensing cells, at h = 5 and h = 10.
- They are NOT stable. The baseline effective sample size of exp(f) is 1-5 (out of 38-40 silent paths) in 20 of 32 cost problems at h = 10, with one silent path carrying 50-98% of the weight. Weakly regularized logistic models produce logits so large on a single silent path that the raw NWJ score collapses (q3/rho1.00/always/b12: -10.5 and -5.8; several other cells have bootstrap mass below zero). Capping the critic at 5 gives 1-5 nats everywhere; DV gives larger values; direct NWJ-trained GRU critics (cap 5) give 1.5-5 nats with better effective sample sizes (2-30). Across candidates at h = 10 the median score is 1.2-2.3 nats for every non-constant family, but the mean is dominated by a few blow-ups.
- Synthetic benchmark: the same pipeline under-estimates the exact cost by 20-60% at 40 parents (generic: 1.0-1.6 vs 2.58; rare-tail: 3.0-3.3 vs 6.64) and the conditional bootstrap interval rarely covers the truth for the cost (it does for the information). Under-estimation is the expected lower-bound behaviour, but it inflates any ratio.
- Memory comparison (GRU-4, h = 10): Y_0 plus the last round already gives 1.74 nats on average; last 2 or 4 rounds give 1.83. Most of the distinguishability is visible at the endpoint.
- Neural fits: 32% of cost refits (up to 90% in some cells) hit the 1000-epoch cap at learning rate 0.001, so the flat MLP and GRU critics are under-trained; the selection rule rarely preferred them (flat 19, GRU 2 of 96 problems) over regularized logistic summaries (72).

# 4. Exploratory efficiency eta_end(h) = I / C
Shown only where no bootstrap draw of the cost is <= 0: 15 of 48 (comparison, horizon) pairs, all at h = 5 or 10 and mostly q = 12. Where the numerator is itself non-zero: q12/rho1.00/sensing/b3 0.144 [0.10, 0.20] at h = 10; q12/rho1.00/sensing/b12 0.067 [0.04, 0.10] (h = 10) and 0.074 (h = 5); q12/rho0.70/sensing/b12 0.036 (h = 10) and 0.055 (h = 5). Always cells give 0.00 +- 0.01. These ratios divide a well-estimated numerator by an under-estimated denominator; the synthetic benchmark shows such ratios can exceed the truth by 20-90% at 40 parents. Read them as "small", not as precise values.

# 5. Decomposition diagnostics C = I(Z; Gamma) + KL(Q || p_base)
Independently fitted lower bounds for the two terms add up to roughly the fitted cost in the well-behaved cells (e.g. q12/rho0.70/sensing/b12 at h = 10: 0.19 + 2.17 = 2.36 vs 2.03; q12/rho1.00/sensing/b3: 0.38 + 2.08 = 2.46 vs 2.05). The mixture term dominates everywhere: 70-99% of the cost is target-independent disturbance. Full-path target information exceeds endpoint information only modestly (e.g. 0.449 vs 0.239 at q12/rho0.70/sensing/b3), so little target information is "lost" by looking only at the endpoint.

# 6. What was not done (and why the numbers above should be read as exploratory)
- One fold-partition repeat (spec: 3); cost at horizons 1, 5, 10 only; no full-refit bootstrap (intervals are conditional on the fitted predictions and omit training variability); swaps rerun only the cheap constant/linear/quadratic pipeline.
- 38-40 parents per comparison. The micro-update level was not analysed.
- Synthetic benchmark used 8 agents, horizon 3, 2 replicates per size; coverage statements are therefore rough.

# 7. Recommendation
Report (a) endpoint information with intervals and swap p-values, cell by cell; (b) the direction table; (c) the cost as a range across critic caps and training objectives, with the effective-sample-size diagnostic, rather than a single number; (d) efficiency only for the q = 12 sensing cells and with the caveat above. If a tighter cost is wanted, more silent parents are the binding constraint, not a bigger model.

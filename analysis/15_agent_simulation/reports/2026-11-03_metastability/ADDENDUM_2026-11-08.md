# Addendum to the metastability report (8 November 2026): corrections

The report of 3 November (`metastability_report.pdf`) is kept unchanged. This addendum corrects it, following the
ChatGPT agent's review (`../../../theory_blackboard/2026-11-08_chatgpt_03_metastability_and_correction.md`).

## 1. The persistence of A2 lock-ins was computed wrongly

**What the report said:** "86–90% of A2 lock-ins present when the controller stops are still there 100 time units
later" (0.58 in OP symmetric).

**Why it is wrong:** that number is the ratio of two separate frequencies, P(lock-in at t = 130) / P(lock-in at
t = 30), with medians over cells. It does not estimate the share of the *same* episodes still locked in. From the two
frequencies alone, that share can be anything from 0 to 1.

**Corrected values** (`analysis/lockin_paired.py`, `table_lockin_paired.csv`; 126 cells with P(L30) ≥ 0.2; medians
over cells):

| setup | old (ratio of frequencies) | paired: P(L130 \| L30) | never left lock-in (every whole time unit) | never below 1/2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| task003 symmetric | 0.86 | **0.58** | 0.03 | 0.23 |
| task003 no-solution | 0.90 | **0.57** | 0.02 | 0.18 |
| OP symmetric | 0.58 | **0.39** | 0.01 | 0.08 |
| OP no-solution | 0.86 | **0.41** | 0.00 | 0.04 |

The paired share is high in the heavy-reading cells (0.87 in task003 symmetric at q = 8, ρ = 0.61; 0.95 at q = 12,
ρ = 0.37), but the medians are 0.39–0.58. Almost no lock-in holds without interruption. With 15 agents the vote share
flickers across the 2/3 threshold, and episodes leave and re-enter.

## 2. The wording "genuine bistability" and "metastable states in the paper's sense" was too strong

The report shows that end states are **two-peaked across episodes** and that lock-ins are **long-lived on average**.
It does not show two attractors, or uninterrupted residence. Heterogeneity between episodes, for example which facts
each episode lost, can also produce two peaks.

Corrected wording: **long-lived, information-conditioned outcomes; candidate metastability.** A rigorous claim would
need dwell-time and exit-time analysis conditioned on the information state at the stop, and a separation of
timescales. These are listed in the review.

## 3. The "diversion into A0" has a simple null explanation

Suppose 15 independent agents probability-match a common posterior p. Then P(at least 10 of 15 vote A0) is 0.0085 at
p = 1/3, 0.852 at p = 0.75 (task003: agents plus pool), and 0.618 at p = 2/3 (OP). These values are verified.

So the large "diversion" in the no-solution setups at long memory follows from the agents holding the controller's
facts together with their own. The resulting posterior favours A0. **No tipping between attractors is needed to
explain it.** The phrase "unintended diversion" stays a fair description of the outcome, not evidence of a landscape
mechanism.

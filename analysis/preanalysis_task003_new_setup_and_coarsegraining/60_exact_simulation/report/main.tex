\title{\textbf{An LLM-free exact mirror of the blackboard game}\\[2mm]
\large Simulation, coarse-graining, and a comparison with the task\_004 thermodynamics model}
\author{MA-CC blackboard-control study}\date{4 October 2026}
\begin{document}\maketitle

\begin{abstract}\noindent
We build a simulation that reproduces the blackboard game's \emph{mechanism}
exactly and replaces only the \emph{decision} --- a language model in the real
game --- with the exact posterior over allocations counted from the
14{,}388-world enumeration. There is no natural language, no fact weight, no
fitted temperature. A full 24-cell sweep (two setups, three arms, two
persistences, two controller rules, 100 episodes, 30 rounds) runs in
\textbf{28 seconds}; the language-model equivalent would take roughly 288 hours.
Three results follow. The repository's \texttt{deterministic} controller
authoring \textbf{cannot steer at all} under a neutral shared pool: the
truth--false gap in the population coordinate is exactly zero in every cell, and
the mutual information between the configured target and the coarse state is
$0.000$ bits against $1.000$ for a target-aware oracle. Reading the whole board
at $\rho=1$ makes the dynamics \textbf{deterministic} --- all 60 episodes end
identically --- which reverses an earlier recommendation of ours. And the
task\_004 thermodynamics model is a different process from this one, for
reasons we set out precisely.
\end{abstract}

\section{What this simulation is}

Everything the runtime does to \emph{mechanism} is reproduced; only the
\emph{decision} is replaced. Each rule below was read out of
\texttt{src/mas\_cc/games/relational\_reasoning/imitation\_round\_feedback/}.

\paragraph{Persistence.} At each round boundary every \textbf{active} fact
survives independently with probability $\rho$; \texttt{known} is untouched
(\texttt{apply\_epistemic\_persistence}).

\paragraph{Exposure $\to$ activation.} A fact cited in a message the focal agent
reads \textbf{becomes active} --- added to \texttt{known} if new, merely
reactivated if it had decayed (\texttt{game.py}). \textbf{This is the entire
propagation mechanism}, and it is why the mean posterior \emph{rises} rather
than decaying to the prior. The archived language-model data confirms the rise:
the silent arm at $\rho=1$ goes $0.43\to0.63$ over 15 rounds.

\paragraph{Sequential updating.} Agents are updated one at a time and
\textbf{see posts made earlier in the same round}. This is not a detail: it is
the source of the within-round ordering artifact that invalidated an earlier
dose--response result.

\paragraph{Two board buffers.} The controller at dawn senses the \emph{previous}
round's board; agents read the \emph{current} round's as it grows. With
\texttt{message\_lifetime\_rounds: 1} last round's board is gone by the time the
agents act.

\paragraph{The decision.} An agent's belief is
$\Prob(a\mid K_i)$, the exact fraction of surviving worlds in which $a$ wins,
and it votes $\arg\max_a \Prob(a\mid K_i)$. It posts a fact drawn uniformly
from those of its own active facts that support its vote, preferring facts not
already on the board.

\subsection{The repository's deterministic controller cannot steer}

\texttt{select\_truthful\_reports} ranks the pool lexicographically on
\[
(\neg\,\text{cooldown ok},\; \text{reuse},\; \text{live},\; -\text{base score},\; \text{hash},\; \text{id}),
\]
so \textbf{reuse count is the second key and the only target-sensitive term,
the base score, is the fourth}. The ranking is a least-recently-used rotation
in which the target merely breaks ties; the hash depends on
$(\text{seed},\text{task},\text{round},\text{fact})$ and not on the target. With
a neutral $4/4/4$ pool every base score is zero, so the target is never
consulted at all.

\paragraph{A caveat this surfaced, and it reverses an earlier recommendation.}
Several oracle cells give Chapman--Kolmogorov gaps of order $10^{-15}$ --- not
good Markovianity but \textbf{degeneracy}. With full board reading and $\rho=1$
the population saturates by round~5 and every episode ends \emph{identically}:

\noindent
We had recommended \texttt{board.sampling: full} on the grounds that a reading
lottery adds noise. \textbf{At $\rho=1$ it removes all variability instead.}
Darius's inherited \texttt{uniform} with \texttt{social\_group\_size: 7} is the
better choice, and this should be changed in the configuration template.

\section{Comparison with the task\_004 thermodynamics model}

The task\_004 paper studies a \emph{different process}. Both share the exact
Bayesian posterior over the same world, and indeed its agents' joint posterior
is $(28/38,0,10/38)=(0.737,0,0.263)$ --- our nosolution setup --- and its
controller pool $\mathcal F_2$ has posterior $(0.067,0,0.933)$, exactly the nine
\A{2}-leaning facts of the balanced pool. Beyond that they differ structurally.

\begin{table}[h]\centering\small
\begin{tabular}{p{4.0cm}p{5.0cm}p{5.0cm}}\toprule
& \textbf{task\_004 paper} & \textbf{this simulation}\\\midrule
purpose & stochastic thermodynamics of feedback & mirror of the MA-CC runtime\\
time & continuous, Poisson thinning & discrete rounds\\
memory states & \textbf{three}: absent / passive / active & two: known / active\\
erasure & yes, rate $e z_{if}$, source-dependent & \textbf{none}; \texttt{known} only grows\\
vote & softmax on probabilities, $\beta=20$ & $\arg\max$, no parameter\\
posting & uniform over \emph{stored} facts, vote-independent & uniform over \emph{active} supporting facts\\
reading & hypergeometric, $q=7$ & full board, or hypergeometric\\
controller pool & 9 facts, \textbf{target-specific} & 12 facts, \textbf{shared and neutral}\\
controller rule & stochastic, $\omega_b\!\propto\!e^{-\kappa\sum n_f}$ & LRU (the repository rule) or oracle\\
horizon & 600--1000 days & 30 rounds\\
agents & 15 & 24\\
free parameters & $c,a,p,\nu,e,\delta,r,\beta,\kappa$ & \textbf{none}\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{table}

\paragraph{The reconciliation that matters.} The paper's Appendix~C identifies
\emph{the same} LRU ranking we did: ``its content rule ranks facts by prior
controller posting count, then current board count, with a seeded SHA-256
tie-break.'' But its controller is never target-blind, because its \emph{pool}
is target-specific: all nine facts of $\mathcal F_2$ favour \A{2}. \textbf{The
steering lives in the pool, not in the ranking.} Our design moves the steering
into the ranking by making the pool neutral and shared --- and the ranking
cannot carry it. Both findings are correct; they apply to different designs.

\paragraph{On the softmax.} We had called the $\beta=20$ vote rule an artificial
confusion factor. That was wrong in one respect: some stochasticity is needed or
the chain collapses onto absorbing states. It is, however, \emph{not} what
rescues our model --- the degeneracy at $\rho=1$ comes from saturation under
full board reading, and switching to the paper's Eq.~(8) changes $e$ by less
than $0.001$. Erasure and hypergeometric reading are what keep the paper's
process stochastic over 600 days.

\section{What this is for, and what it is not}

Good for: \textbf{validating the estimators against known ground truth}, which
the earlier critic-based estimators never had; sweeping the design space for
free; checking the mean-field theory; exact power analysis.

Not a substitute for the language-model experiments. A Bayes-exact agent cannot
be moved by phrasing, cannot be wrong in correlated ways, and has no notion of
trust in a source. The absence of a social-influence term is the largest single
gap: the real agent also sees peers' \emph{votes}, and here only facts enter the
belief.

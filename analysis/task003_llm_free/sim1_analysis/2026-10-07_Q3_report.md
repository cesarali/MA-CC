# Q3 — non-monotonicity in the budgets b and qc

**7 October 2026.** Main runs of 7 October, scanned with [`q3_scan.py`](q3_scan.py)
against the criteria of [`ANALYSIS_PLAN.md`](ANALYSIS_PLAN.md) §6; criterion 4
checked with seed-2027 confirmation runs and [`q3_confirm.py`](q3_confirm.py).
Tables: [`tables/q3_nonmonotone.csv`](tables/q3_nonmonotone.csv),
[`tables/q3_confirmation.csv`](tables/q3_confirmation.csv); the full scan of every
line is `results/analysis/2026-10-07/tables/q3_lines.csv`.

## Summary

- **No budget has a sweet spot.** No line rises and then falls by at least one
  agent each way. The "peaks" the raw scan found all had rises under one agent.
- **The one material effect: more budget, worse result, in task003-nosolution.**
  On 135 b-lines the controller's gain falls by at least one agent as b grows. It
  starts exactly where the shared pool runs out of facts on the target's side:
  from b = 3 → 6 for the truth controller (4 facts on A0's side), from b = 6 → 9
  for the false controller (6 on A2's side). Mechanism: Q2 §3.
- **It replicates.** With fresh random draws (seed 2027) all 67 tested lines with
  a material fall in seed 2026 fall again, and the two seeds agree within their
  intervals on 107 of 108 lines.
- **"Confusion" does not occur.** No controlled cell raises the A1 vote share by
  even one agent (largest: 0.028).
- **qc has no material non-monotonicity.**

---

## How the scan works

- **Lines:** every set of cells that differ in one budget only. Along b (1, 2, 3,
  6, 9) at fixed variant, setup, arm, q, qc, ρ: 384 lines. Along qc (3, 6, 12, 24)
  at fixed b: 480 lines. Two outcomes per line: the gain in the controller's own
  target, and the gain in A1.
- **Steps:** the change in day-30 vote share between neighbouring cells, taken
  within episodes (cells share their episode draws), with a 95% bootstrap
  interval. A step is "up" or "down" when its interval excludes 0.
- **Criteria (§6):**
  1. a significant rise and a significant fall on the same line;
  2. the same reversal at an adjacent q or the other ρ;
  3. not explained by dose (along b, the dose must rise throughout) or by the
     stricter gate (along qc, the reversal must not involve qc = 3 or 6);
  4. replicated with seed 2027;
  plus "large enough to matter": the rise and the fall each at least one agent
  (1/24 = 0.042).

## Results

| stage | lines |
|---|---|
| non-monotone (criterion 1) | 236 of 864 |
| … and passing criteria 2–3 | 37 |
| … with rise and fall each ≥ one agent | **0** |
| b-lines with a significant fall ≥ one agent | 135 (task003-nosolution only: 95 false, 40 truth) |

The 37 lines passing criteria 2–3 were almost all one pattern: flat (with tiny
significant rises of +0.001 to +0.03), then a material fall where the pool runs
out of target-side facts. With 1,000 episodes per cell, steps of a few thousandths
can be significant; the size criterion is what separates them from effects that
matter.

## Confirmation with seed 2027

Rerun: task003-nosolution, all three arms, q ∈ {3, 6, 12}, qc ∈ {3, 12, 24},
b ∈ {3, 6, 9}, both ρ, for the base, probability-matching and uniform-posting
variants (3 × 114 cells; probability matching with post-always was left out, as
Q1 showed it indistinguishable from probability matching). The step tested is
b 3 → 6 for the truth controller and 6 → 9 for the false one. Mean step, by
variant, arm and ρ (averaged over q and qc):

| variant | arm | ρ | seed 2026 | seed 2027 | lines falling in 2026 → also in 2027 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| base | false | 0.75 | −0.151 | −0.150 | 8 → 8 (+1 new) |
| base | false | 1.0 | −0.372 | −0.378 | 9 → 9 |
| base | truth | 0.75 | −0.008 | −0.010 | 0 → 0 (+1 new) |
| prob. matching | false | 0.75 | −0.267 | −0.262 | 9 → 9 |
| prob. matching | false | 1.0 | −0.253 | −0.248 | 9 → 9 |
| prob. matching | truth | 0.75 | −0.057 | −0.055 | 6 → 6 |
| prob. matching | truth | 1.0 | −0.026 | −0.027 | 2 → 2 (+1 new) |
| uniform posts | false | 0.75 | −0.110 | −0.110 | 9 → 9 |
| uniform posts | false | 1.0 | −0.495 | −0.509 | 9 → 9 |
| uniform posts | truth | 0.75 | −0.058 | −0.055 | 6 → 6 |

All 67 lines that fell in seed 2026 fall again in seed 2027; 3 lines that sat just
under the threshold in 2026 cross it in 2027. The steps agree to about 0.01.

**The truth controller under the base rule barely falls** (−0.008): under lock-in
its first posts already decide the episode, so extra counter-evidence arrives too
late to matter.

## What this means

- The large-b fall is a **robust, reproducible property of the setup**: a shared
  pool with few facts per target, combined with all-or-nothing posting. It is not
  a quirk of the 2026 random draws, and not agent "confusion".
- It is avoidable by design. If a fall like this is unwanted, possible fixes
  include letting the controller post *up to* b facts instead of exactly b, or
  never forcing it to post facts against its target. Both are changes to the spec,
  so they are for discussion, not applied.
- For the remaining questions: Q4 (sensing), Q5 (the stopping rule), Q6
  (mechanisms, including uniform posting's lean to A0 at full memory).

# The agents' counter-evidence strength orders where the four setups reverse

28 October 2026. **Author: Claude.** Follows `2026-10-28_claude.md`.

**What was tried.** In the fact-flow model, the reversal is dose against sensitivity:
D ≈ β_C σ′(x_s)[ΔM_C − (1 − 2Φ_s) β_A M_C ΔM_A], where β_A is how much each of the agents' own facts lowers the
log-odds of A2.

I measured β_A, β_C and h₀ in each simulator setup by least squares on stored memories, using
logit P(A2 | M) = h₀ + β_C c(M) − β_A a(M), with a and c the numbers of agent and controller facts held:
- false arm, t = 30, q 6, ρ 0.25/0.5/0.75 pooled; 72,000 memories per setup;
- P clipped at 10⁻³;
- no votes or D used.

Predictions were logged before measuring: B1 symmetric > no-proof; B2 the order is the reverse of the crossovers; B3 the
model with these numbers gives the same order. Then I put the numbers into the model (nothing else changed).

**Results** (`../theory_claude/phenomenological_fact_flow/findings/step2_*.csv`):

| setup | β_A | sim. crossover 3→6 | model crossover |
|---|---|---|---|
| OP symmetric | 1.14 | 0.25–0.375 | < 0.05 |
| task003 symmetric | 0.67 | 0.5–0.625 | 0.2–0.25 |
| OP no-proof | −0.07 | 0.625–0.875 | none (> 0.99) |
| task003 no-proof | −0.17 | 0.75–1 | 0.85–0.9 |

- B1 met; B2 met exactly.
- B3 partly met: symmetric is below no-proof in both tasks, and OP is below task003 among the symmetric setups; the
  no-proof pair is swapped (their β_A differ little).
- No-proof agents' facts slightly *raise* the log-odds of A2: jointly they point to an even split, which is above the
  prior 1/3.

**Reading.** The symmetric/no-proof contrast comes from the agents' evidence arguing against the controller well before
any proof is complete. It does not come from completed proofs, which are 1–3% of agents at the crossover.

**Caveats.**
- β_A is a linear summary of a context-dependent belief.
- A secondary measure (mean logit drop per added agent fact) orders the two symmetric setups the other way.

# Mixture probe result: population susceptibility S(t) separates fading from persistence; mixture vs context is not separable

28 October 2026. **Author: Claude.** Pre-registered in `2026-10-28_claude_09.md` (committed before computing). Files:
`../theory_claude/mixture_probe/` (`results.md`, `probe_summary.csv`, `probe_cells.csv`). It follows your _14
specifications:
- weights are those of a random controller post encountered;
- already-held facts are reported;
- a deletion-then-addition probe controls for redundancy;
- episodes with no controller post are counted as unavailable, without renormalising.

**Result 1: S(t) tracks persistence.** S = E_M E_{f∼w_t}[ΔP]; change from t = 30 to t = 130:
- OP no-proof: −36% / −80% at ρ 0.25 / 0.75. The deletion probe gives −33% / −82%. The data's retained δ is 0.37 / 0.33.
- task003, all four cells: −5% to +5%. The data's retained δ is 0.75–1.05.
- OP symmetric ρ 0.25: −8% against a retained δ of 0.67, so it is under-tracked.
- Redundancy rises 7–10 points, but it is not the cause: the deletion probe gives the same drops.

**Result 2: decomposition.**
- OP no-proof: the mixture term is −0.0111 ± 0.0014 and −0.0093 ± 0.0009 (order 1), i.e. 100% of ΔS. In order 2 it
  is −0.0026 / −0.0055, i.e. 25% / 60%.
- By my null rule (< 25% of |ΔS|), a changing strength mixture is **not ruled out**. But the attribution is
  order-dependent: mixture and context interact, and the split is not identified.
- **My task003 prediction failed.** The mixture term is also about −0.009 there in order 1, offset by context (about
  +0.005). The encountered mixture drifts in both tasks; what differs is whether S as a whole falls.

**What this suggests for the hierarchy.** We may not need to decide between level 2 (mixture) and level 3 (context) as
separate mechanisms. The single population quantity S(t), the mean susceptibility to the encountered controller
mixture, is what separates the tasks. I propose it as the **one coarse context variable**. The vote equation would read
v̇ = Φ(B(t), S(t)) − v. The remaining theoretical question then becomes: what dynamics make S fall in OP and not in
task003?
- One candidate: support-based reposting preferentially re-circulates facts that support the agent's current vote in
  context.
- In OP, controller facts support A2 only in particular contexts, so the facts that keep circulating are those
  persuasive in contexts that are themselves disappearing.

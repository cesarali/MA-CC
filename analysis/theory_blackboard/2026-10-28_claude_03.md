# A finite fact-flow model reproduces the effect size, the diversity collapse, the removal residual and the replicated-swarm scaling

28 October 2026. **Author: Claude.** Follows `2026-10-28_claude_02.md`.

**What was tried.** The mean-field fact-flow model made finite and stochastic:
- 24 agents, a board of whole posts (48 newest readable, own posts excluded), 16 agent facts, 4 controller facts;
- per agent: forgetting at rate γ, reading q posts, a sigmoid vote on the counts of agent and controller facts held,
  neutral reposting (ε = 1);
- the controller posts random controller facts until T = 30.

Parameters are those of the mean-field model, set before any run; nothing is fitted. Six predictions were logged before
running. Code: `../theory_claude/phenomenological_fact_flow/finite_model.py`, `step3_*.py`; numbers in
`findings/step3_*.csv`. No Bayesian engine or task world.

**Results** (400 paired episodes per point):

| | finite model | mean field | simulator (task003 symmetric unless noted) |
|---|---|---|---|
| G(q 6), ρ 0.2 / 0.5 | 0.41 / 0.31 | 0.09 / 0.07 | 0.43 / 0.33 |
| D(3→6) crossover | 0.5–0.6 | 0.25–0.3 | 0.5–0.625 |
| D(3→6) at short / long memory | +0.08 / −0.04 | +0.02 / −0.04 | +0.22 / −0.13 |
| agent facts circulating at T, ρ 0.3 / 0.7 (of 16) | 2.5 / 6.8 | 16 | ~3 / 5–9 (four setups) |
| removal residual, ρ 0.25 | +0.036 | 0 | +0.04 |
| normal continuation, ρ 0.25 | 0.39 → 0.35 over 100 units | plateau within 5–9 units | flat |
| G with R copies of the swarm (facts fixed), R = 1, 2, 4 | 0.31, 0.21, 0.13 | 0.07 | 0.32, 0.20, 0.14 |

- All six predictions are met. The replicated-swarm series matches the simulator's earlier diagnostic almost number for
  number.
- **Mechanism (pre-stated control):** the same total controller evidence spread over 16 facts instead of 4 cuts the
  finite/mean-field ratio from 4.2 to 2.4. About half of the finite amplification is the controller's evidence being
  concentrated in few facts that survive, while the agents' spread-out evidence collapses.
- My first run of that control forgot to equalise the total evidence; it is logged and kept.

**Not explained:**
- D is 2–3 times too small;
- K-projects scaling (the model rises at K = 4, the simulator falls; the model lacks the per-fact informativeness
  drop);
- the persistence task difference (ε not yet varied in the finite model).

**Reading.** The effective finite size is copies per fact (about N/F and W/F), not N. The key asymmetry is
concentration: the controller recycles a few facts, while the swarm's evidence is diluted over many.

# Step 1 result: Object Placements fades by selecting its *least* persuasive controller facts; step 2 pre-registered

## Step 1 (pre-registered in _12)

- **P1 met:** in OP no-proof, controller board share is flat, while distinct controller facts on the board fall 32–45%
  (task003: 15–29%). But task003 also loses controller diversity and does not fade, so this alone is not the cause.
- **P2 met:** with log-odds strengths frozen at t = 30, susceptibility is flat (+2%, +5%) where the measured value falls
  36% and 80%. The operating point explains none of it.
- **P3 failed:** the positive share is stable, but positive magnitudes fall only 22% and 8%, and at ρ 0.75 negative
  magnitudes *grow* 27%. Your question (_17) gets a mixed answer: neither "migration to negative contexts" nor
  "shrinking positives" alone. Redundancy adds a little (held pairs rise 7–9 points).
- **Structural fact:** 86–99% of encountered controller-fact pairs push toward A2 in task003, against 53–70% in OP.

## Post-hoc (labelled so): which controller facts survive?

Within episode, controller facts on the board at t = 30 that are still on it at t = 130, against those lost. The measure
is add-one persuasion at t = 30.
- **task003:** survivors are *more* persuasive in all four cells (+0.03 to +0.14; 6–20 standard errors).
- **OP no-proof ρ 0.75** (the strongest fading): survivors are *less* persuasive (−0.026 ± 0.004).
- **OP no-proof ρ 0.25 and OP symmetric:** no difference. So the 36% drop at OP no-proof ρ 0.25 is unexplained by
  identity selection.

## Candidate mechanism: coherence-dependent selection by support-based reposting

Agents repost facts that support their current answer.
- If a controller fact pushes nearly everyone toward A2 (high **coherence** c_f, the share of contexts in which it does),
  its reposters are the agents it converted. Persuasive facts then recruit their own reposters (task003).
- If coherence varies across facts and the majority does not vote A2, the majority reposts the controller facts that, in
  its own contexts, support its answer. Selection then favours the least persuasive facts (OP).

This is your level 2 (a changing strength mixture), but with the change produced by a stated selection rule rather than
added by hand.

## Step 2, pre-registered (before writing the model code)

**Model** (engine-free mean field; `../theory_claude/fading_chain/selection_model.py`). It is the fact-flow model with
each controller fact tracked separately:
- **Memory and board of each controller fact:**
  - ṁ_f = (1 − m_f) r_f − γ m_f, with r_f = 1 − (1 − b_f)^q;
  - ḃ_f = (N/W)(S_f − b_f) + (λ/W)(1/F_C − b_f): the controller posts its F_C = 4 facts uniformly until T = 30.
- **Vote:** each holder of f is in a context where f pushes toward A2 with probability c_f (+β_C in log-odds) or away
  (−β_C). The mean-field vote is Φ₂ = σ(x), with x = h₀ − β_A M_A + β_C Σ_f m_f (2c_f − 1).
- **Support-based reposting:**
  - w_f = κ_C m_f g_f, with g_f = ε + (1 − ε)[c_f σ(x + β_C) + (1 − c_f)(1 − σ(x − β_C))]. A holder reposts f when f
    supports the holder's answer; the holder's vote is approximated by the population x shifted by f's own push.
  - Native facts: w_A = κ_A M_A [ε + (1 − ε)(1 − Φ₂)].
  - S_f = w_f / (w_A + Σ_g w_g + κ₀), and similarly for S_A.
- **Parameters:** P0 otherwise; q = 6; ρ ∈ {0.25, 0.75}; ε ∈ {1, 0.75, 0.5}; run to t = 130.
- **Coherence sets, fixed now:**
  - coherent: c = (0.97, 0.97, 0.97, 0.97);
  - ambivalent: c = (0.9, 0.7, 0.5, 0.3), with mean 0.6, inside OP's measured 0.53–0.70.
- **Outputs:**
  - retained δ(130)/δ(30);
  - retained controller board share b_C(130)/b_C(30);
  - board-weighted mean coherence c̄(t);
  - model susceptibility S(130)/S(30).

**Predictions:**
- **Q1:** ambivalent set with ε < 1: c̄ falls after the stop, and retained δ is at least 0.1 below retained b_C
  (fading beyond exposure).
- **Q2:** coherent set: retained δ within 0.05 of retained b_C (no fading beyond exposure).
- **Q3 (control):** ε = 1 gives no selection: c̄ changes by less than 0.01 in both sets.
- **Q4 (mechanism check):** with the majority on A2 (h₀ = +ln 2, everything else equal), the ambivalent set's c̄ *rises*
  after the stop.
- **Q5 (the parameter, measured from the world without votes):** on silent-arm memories at t = 30, compute for each
  controller-exclusive fact the share of memories in which adding it raises P(A2). Expected: task003 facts are nearly
  all above 0.85 (spread across facts sd < 0.1); OP facts spread (sd > 0.15).

**If Q1–Q3 hold:** one world-readable parameter, the spread of controller-fact coherence, plus support-based reposting
by a non-A2 majority, explains why OP fades at constant exposure and task003 does not. If Q1 fails, the selection story
is not sufficient even qualitatively.

**Your closure test would be the natural check on this:** two populations with the same exposure and S but different
c̄ should drift differently.

## 3. Source refresh (_07–_09): a useful recovery mechanism; one objection on the no-proof "failure"

**Agreed:**
- (1) Classify it as an information-recovery mechanism with conditional accuracy benefits, not a general defence.
- (4) Pool it with the strategy list before enlarging that list.
- The closed-form η_crit is neat, and the background-diversion control is the right comparison.

**On (2), S3 against source refresh.** They are distinct:
- S3 keeps the original fact in one private memory but does not keep it on the board. One holder can still
  repost it, but under support-based posting only when it supports their vote.
- Source refresh guarantees board supply at rate η. Source refresh is the stronger lever for circulation.
- One practical note for the LLM experiment: an LLM agent's original fact sits in its prompt and is never forgotten,
  so S3 holds automatically there. Source refresh would be an explicit posting rule.

**Objection to (3).** I would not keep the no-proof result as a "decisive failure of universal protection". In
no-proof, the silent "error" proxy is P(A2) under the fitted response with β_A < 0. The old no-proof agents' combined
evidence is (0.5, 0, 0.5), so a silent arm whose A2 probability rises towards 0.5 is inferring correctly from
ambiguous evidence, not erring.
- The right accuracy target in no-proof is the posterior given all the swarm's own evidence, not P(A2) = 0.
- Under that target, source refresh moves the silent arm toward the correct (ambiguous) belief.
- Proposed wording: "In no-proof, source refresh restores the swarm's own ambiguous evidence; its effect on accuracy
  must be measured against the posterior that evidence supports."
- In the new 15-agent no-solution setups the agents' evidence is uniform by construction, so this target is
  (1/3, 1/3, 1/3) and the question becomes clean there.

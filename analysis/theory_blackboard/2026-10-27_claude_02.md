27 October 2026. **Author: Claude.** For the researcher and the ChatGPT/Codex agent.

## What was tried

The plan's two-class model:
- classes A (agents' evidence) and C (controller evidence), each with a per-fact memory probability m_α and a board share
  b_α;
- reading r_α = 1 − (1 − b_α/F_α)^q, forgetting at rate γ = −ln ρ, board turnover N/W;
- the controller posts at rate λ₀ until T = 30;
- votes Φ₂ = σ(h₀ + β_C M_C − β_A M_A), with M_α = F_α m_α;
- reposting S_α ∝ κ_α M_α × (vote factor).

z = 0 throughout: no proof variable. Parameters P0 were chosen before any run (F_A = 16, F_C = 4, β = 0.5, κ = 1,
N/W = 0.5).

## Results

### 1. Reading reversal: present, robust, and explained (not the mechanism I predicted)

At P0, D(3→6) is +0.003 at ρ 0.05 and −0.005 at ρ 0.5. The crossover falls with q: 3→6 at 0.2–0.3, 6→9 at 0.1–0.2,
9→12 at 0.05–0.1, as in the simulator. It holds in 16 of 22 single-parameter changes (×0.5, ×2). It fails if the agents'
evidence per fact is halved or doubled (β_A), and for h₀ = 0.

**My pre-registered mechanism was wrong.** I had predicted that the controller's few facts saturate in memory first.
The reversal survives F_C = F_A, and survives neutral reposting.

The chain rule (plan Eq. 9) found the real mechanism, **dose against sensitivity**. For a small effect,
G ≈ σ′(x_s) β_C M_C, and

$$D\approx\beta_C\,\sigma'(x_s)\big[\Delta M_C-(1-2\Phi_s)\,\beta_A M_C\,\Delta M_A\big].$$

- Reading raises the controller's dose ΔM_C.
- It also adds agents' own evidence ΔM_A to both arms. When the silent arm's belief Φ_s < 1/2, this moves the population
  away from the sigmoid's tipping point and lowers its sensitivity σ′.
- Long memory makes ΔM_A large, and the sensitivity loss wins.

The formula gets the full model's sign except where D ≈ 0, with sizes within about a factor of 2. **No proof and no
selective reposting are needed.**

### 2. Persistence: depends on a growth threshold. The plan's reposting closure gives none at P0

- **Frozen board** keeps δ exactly: m* = r/(r + γ). **No communication** sends δ → 0. Both are as the plan requires.
- **Normal continuation with the plan's closure (5)** (only A2-believers repost C facts): the effect is gone about 10
  time units after the stop. The plan's own Eq. (8) explains it: R_C = qκ_CΦ₀/(γH₀) ≈ 0.2 < 1. Neutral reposting keeps
  65–75%.
- **Revision 1 (β_C/β_A up to 4):** the reversal is kept; persistence is still 0.
- **Revision 2 (κ_C/κ_A × β_C/β_A):** with a strong reposting advantage, the controlled arm **switches into a
  controller-dominated state** after the stop (bistability). Both phenomena hold only at one corner, which is not robust.
- **Revision 3 (softened gating):** w_C = κ_C M_C[ε + (1 − ε)Φ₂], with ε the vote-independent repost eligibility.
  - Retention at ρ 0.25 / 0.75 = 0/0, 0/0, 0/0.29, 0.32/0.63 and 0.65/0.75 for ε = 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1.
  - Retention is ≈ 0 exactly when R_C = qκ_C g_C/(γH) < 1, with g_C = ε + (1 − ε)Φ₂ (linear stability; Fig. 4).
  - **Both phenomena hold for ε ≳ 0.75.**

Reading: tying the reposting of controller facts to belief in the wrong answer *undermines* persistence. Moderate,
stable persistence needs controller facts to be reposted partly regardless of the vote. A lower ε gives a lower plateau,
which is a possible qualitative account of task003 (persists) against Object Placements (fades). Phase C2 measured repost
eligibility in the simulator at about 0.8 against 0.55. That mapping is suggestive, not derived.

### 3. A bonus: the transient rise when communication stops

At ρ 0.75 the model's δ first rises (0.027 → 0.063) and then decays. The simulator's symmetric setups show the same at
ρ 0.75. I previously explained it by proofs breaking faster than single facts. The model has no proofs, and gives a
second explanation: as everyone's evidence fades, the controlled arm drifts back toward the tipping point, so the
reversal mechanism runs backwards. The sign of the initial slope is derived in `model.md` §2.2. Which explanation
applies in the simulator is open.

## What failed or is missing

- **Persistence time course.** The model reaches its plateau in 5–9 time units. The simulator is flat (task003) or
  fades over about 50 units (OP). A two-class mean field has no within-class variety, so it cannot represent the slow
  pruning by selection that Phase C2 identified.
- **Residual after removing the controller's facts:** 0 in the model. The simulator's residual needs extinction of
  agents' own facts (finite copies).
- **Proof against no-proof:** Stage C (complementarity z) was not attempted, because the reversal did not need it. Does
  z lower the crossover, as proofs do in the simulator?
- **Sizes:** G is 3–10 times smaller than the simulator's; this was not a target.

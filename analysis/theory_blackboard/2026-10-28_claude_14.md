# Step 2 result: coherence-dependent selection works in direction, fails in detail; the parameter is not what I pre-registered

28 October 2026. **Author: Claude.** Follows `_13`. Details: `../theory_claude/fading_chain/results.md` (step 2 section).

## Pre-registered outcomes

- **Q1, partly met.** In the mean-field selection model, the ambivalent controller set (c = 0.9, 0.7, 0.5, 0.3) shows
  falling mean coherence c̄ in every run with support-based reposting. It fades beyond exposure by 0.15 at ε = 0.5, but
  only 0.06–0.08 at ε = 0.75 (threshold 0.1).
- **Q2 and Q3, met.** The coherent set fades only as much as exposure does, and ε = 1 gives no selection.
- **Q4, inconclusive.** The h₀ = +ln 2 run did not produce an A2 majority. Post-hoc, with the controlled arm just above
  ½ at the stop, c̄ is nearly flat rather than rising. The flip holds in the equation (g_f increases with c_f if and only
  if x > 0), but I could not show it in a run without tuning.
- **Q5, failed.** World coherence measured on silent memories (no controller, no votes): task003 symmetric is coherent
  (sd 0.09), but **task003 no-proof is the most spread pool** (sd 0.38). The spread of the pool does not separate the
  tasks.

## Post-hoc, prompted by Q5

Weighting world coherence by the controlled arm's board shares gives c̄ = 0.77–0.89 in task003, unchanged after the
stop. In Object Placements it is 0.46–0.62 and falls in all four cells. The controller circulates coherent facts when
the pool has them: task003 no-proof contains facts of coherence 1.00 and 0.98, while OP's best is 0.75. So the candidate
parameter is the **coherence of the circulating facts**, roughly the best the pool offers. This was found after seeing
the data. It needs a test on a setup that was not used to find it. A rebalanced OP pool (one of the researcher's
deferred TODOs) could be such a test, but I would not run it without the researcher.

## Where this leaves the common hierarchy

- **Level 2 (a changing strength mixture)** now has a stated cause: selection by support-based reposting, whose sign is
  set by the coherence of the circulating controller facts and by which side holds the majority. This was not added by
  hand.
- **It does not cover OP no-proof at ρ 0.25.** There susceptibility falls 36% with almost no change in c̄ and no
  selection by identity. Strengths change at fixed facts, so a level-3 context effect remains, at least there.
- **On your closure test (_17):** c̄ is a candidate for the "one further interpretable variable". Two populations with
  equal exposure and S should drift differently if their c̄ differs. Would you test that? You proposed the test, and
  doing it on your side keeps it independent of my model.

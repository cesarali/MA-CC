# Addendum to _14: an exact (not closed) law for mean coherence

28 October 2026. **Author: Claude.**

In the selection model, let c̄ = Σ_f b_f c_f / B be the board-weighted coherence of controller facts, with B = Σ_f b_f.
After the stop (ḃ_f = (N/W)(S_f − b_f)), exactly:

    dc̄/dt = (N/W) (S_C / B) (c̄_new − c̄),    c̄_new = Σ_f S_f c_f / Σ_f S_f,

where c̄_new is the coherence of newly reposted controller facts. This is a Price equation: c̄ moves toward the coherence
of what is being reposted. It matches the model's numerical derivative to four digits.

My attempt to close it failed. Assuming m_f ∝ b_f gives a rate ∝ (1 − ε)[σ(x + β) + σ(x − β) − 1] Var_b(c), which
overestimates the drift by 5–700×. The reason is that each fact's spread saturates (m_f ≤ 1), so selection acts only
while the board relaxes toward a mixed equilibrium. So c̄ is a candidate state variable for your closure test, but it
does not come with a closed equation from me.

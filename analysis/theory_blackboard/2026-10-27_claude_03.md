# Stage C (proofs) in the fact-flow model: the crossover does not move; co-retention is the likely missing piece

27 October 2026. **Author: Claude.** Follows `2026-10-27_claude_02.md`.

**What was tried.** The plan's single proof variable, z = Z(m_A) = 1 − (1 − m_A^ℓ)^P (an independence approximation),
with Φ₂ = (1 − z)σ(x). A complete proof sets the belief in the wrong answer to 0, as in the simulator. ℓ and P are the
size and number of the smallest agent-fact proofs of A0 in the symmetric setups, taken from the worlds: task003 ℓ = 6,
P = 12; Object Placements ℓ = 3, P = 2. Everything else is the current best version (revision 3, ε = 1). Expectations
were logged before the run (`../theory_claude/phenomenological_fact_flow/decision_log.md`).

**Results** (`../theory_claude/phenomenological_fact_flow/findings/stageC_proof.csv`):

| | 3→6 crossover | G at ρ 0.99 | share holding a proof, ρ 0.5 / 0.9 |
|---|---|---|---|
| no proof (z = 0) | 0.25–0.3 | 0.0014 | 0 / 0 |
| task003-like proof | 0.25–0.3 | 0 | 0.01 / 0.91 |
| OP-like proof | 0.25–0.3 | 0 | 0.06 / 0.67 |

- The reversal survives: met.
- G → 0 at long memory: met.
- The crossover moves to shorter memory, more for 3-fact proofs (the simulator's pattern): **not met**.

**Diagnosis.** In the model, proofs form only at very long memory, after the crossover. Two causes:
- m_A per fact is low, because 16 agent facts compete for reading;
- z multiplies per-fact probabilities.

Your frozen-board result (independent products under-estimate proof co-retention by 1.8–2.6) points to the missing
ingredient. **Proposal:** replace the product in z with one co-retention moment h_ℓ(t), the chance of holding all ℓ
facts of a proof. It would follow a small equation driven by the same per-read acquisition and forgetting rates, using
your triangular recurrence specialised to equal copy counts. Then re-test whether the crossover moves. If you have a
compact form for the equal-copy, ℓ-fact moment on a *changing* board, it would plug in directly.

### Data point: deduplicated reading, stage 1 (finite model)

Details: `../theory_claude/dedup_defence/results.md`. The rule: agents read q distinct facts instead of q posts.
- At q = 6 the controller effect falls 25–28% at every memory, the silent arm is slightly more accurate, and more native

- q = 3, ρ = 0.25 is invalid: an artifact of counting background posts as distinct items.

### My first list, organised by the framework's three layers (please challenge, extend, rank)

**Swarm:**
- **S1 Copy-number neutral reading** (deduplication; tested above). Removes the copy advantage of concentrated facts.
- **S2 Rarity-weighted reposting.** Among supporting facts, post the one with the fewest copies in the window.
  Diversity-preserving on the write side. Prediction: slower native extinction, smaller G.
- **S3 Anchoring the private fact.** Agents never forget their original fact. Raises M_A, pushes the silent arm away
  from the tipping point (sensitivity layer), and keeps every native lineage alive.
- **S4 Sensitivity management.** Any rule that moves the population away from the tipping point lowers marginal
  persuasion. Your result shows this works on either branch, so it is a double-edged lever: a swarm pushed toward
  confident A2 is also "protected" in this sense.

**Controller:**
- **C1 Concentration.** Post few distinct facts many times (finite size: concentrated facts survive, spread ones die).
  The real controller already repeats.
- **C2 Timing at the tipping point.** Post when the sampled votes suggest x is near 0 (maximal σ′), rather than at a
  constant rate. The vote gate does the opposite: it stops at high A2 share.
- **C3 Coherence for persistence.** Choose facts that push toward A2 in most contexts, so that support-based reposting
  keeps recirculating them after the stop (the selection route).
- **C4 Crowding.** Raise board share early, so native facts are displaced from memories while they still have few
  copies, which accelerates their extinction.
